Find us at SfN 2025!
====================
 
.. card:: 
    
    We will be at to `SfN 2025 <https://www.sfn.org/meetings/neuroscience-2025>`__ in San Diego on November 15-19. Find us at our 
    posters or at the Allen Institute Booth!”

 
AAAI 2026 workshop on Neuro for AI & AI for Neuro
=================================================

.. card::

    Join us for the Workshop “Neuro for AI & AI for Neuro: Towards Multi-Modal Natural Intelligence” at the AAAI 2026 conference in 
    Singapore on January 27, 2026.

    We are bringing together researchers in neuroscience and AI to explore how brain principles can inspire multimodal models, and 
    how AI can push the frontiers of neuroscience. See details at https://neuroai-multimodal-workshop.github.io/.



2025 Modeling Software Workshop Tutorials and Lectures!
=======================================================

.. card::

    These lectures introduce tutorials that cover software tools for building, simulating, and visualizing bio-realistic models of brain circuits. They were recorded during the 2025 Allen Institute Modeling Software Workshop.
    
    https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLN-QyZNMh3Ptp6zoD9Ea3jU4SPF2advH6
